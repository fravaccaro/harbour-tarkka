Sailfish OS

Tarkka

A digital magnifying glass for Sailfish OS - zoom, filter and light what you are looking at, with one hand.

Documentation GitHub
Releases OpenRepos Translate Donate

What you can do

  • Smooth digital zoom up to 4x
  • Colour filters: grayscale, negative, whiteboard, high-contrast pairs
  • Show, hide and reorder the filters you actually use
  • Adjust brightness and contrast on the live view
  • Turn on the torch when the light is poor
  • A minimalist layout built for one-handed use
